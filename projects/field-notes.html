Editorial system · Campaign

Field Notes

A visual language for noticing the details that shape a place.

Client Field NotesYear 2022Scope Editorial, campaign, identity

The challenge

Make the
ordinary vivid.

Field Notes began as a small editorial series about material, craft, and everyday observation. The project had a strong voice, but no consistent visual world to connect each issue, photograph, and event.

We set out to create an identity that felt collected rather than constructed — a system with room for texture, surprise, and the marks of the people who use it.

Our approach

Process is
part of the story.

The identity is built from a set of simple gestures: hand-drawn forms, flexible grids, and an intentionally tactile relationship between image and type. Instead of hiding the process, the system makes it visible — turning each edition into a new composition within a recognizable family.
