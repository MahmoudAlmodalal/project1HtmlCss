Brand identity · Digital platform

Northstar

Giving a new generation of explorers a clearer way forward.

Client Northstar Year 2024 Scope Strategy, identity, digital

The challenge

Find the signal.
Build the path.

Northstar came to us with a clear ambition and a crowded category. Their product helped people make better decisions, but the existing identity was too quiet to communicate its confidence or its sense of possibility.

We needed to create a brand that could feel intelligent without becoming cold, and directional without losing its human edge.

Our approach

A visual system
with momentum.

We built the identity around a simple idea: the best direction is the one that helps you move. A precise wordmark, a high-contrast palette, and a modular constellation of shapes gave the brand a system that could stretch from a launch film to a responsive product experience.
