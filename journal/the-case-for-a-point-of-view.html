The case for a point of view in every project.

Direction is not a mood board. It is the small, durable belief that helps a team decide what belongs.

03

Field note

Every project begins with a long list of possibilities. A point of view is what keeps possibility from turning into drift. It gives the work a reason to choose one path and leave another behind.

It does not need to be dramatic. Often it starts with a small observation: people are tired of being spoken to in jargon; the category has become too polished; the most useful thing about the product has gone unnoticed. The observation becomes valuable when it changes how a team behaves.

A point of view is a filter.

Good creative direction has a practical quality. It helps a team decide what to say yes to. It tells us whether an image feels too expected, whether a headline sounds like everyone else, or whether a new feature deserves the same attention as the central promise.

Without that filter, projects tend to accumulate. Every stakeholder contributes another good idea, and soon the work becomes a collection of reasonable choices with no recognizable shape. A point of view does not make those choices easy, but it makes the conversation more honest.

“The goal is not to be different for its own sake. The goal is to be recognizably yourself on purpose.”

Commitment creates character.

There is a common fear that a strong opinion will exclude people. In practice, the opposite is often true. When a brand knows what it stands for, the right people can recognize themselves in it more quickly. They do not need to decode every message because the work carries a consistent posture.

That posture can be warm, precise, irreverent, generous, or quietly ambitious. The word matters less than the choices it produces. A point of view should show up in the smallest details: the pace of a page, the way an interface guides someone, the things a brand refuses to over-explain.

Creative work earns its character through commitment. Make the observation. Name the belief. Then let it do the useful work of guiding everything that follows.
